# Surfer assets: sources and licences

| Asset | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Body meshes and skin weights (`female.glb`, `male.glb`, `grommet.glb`) | Generated with MPFB 2.0.17 (MakeHuman) in Blender 5.2.2 LTS from MPFB's data: the base mesh, the macro targets (gender, age, muscle, weight, proportions, height), the face targets listed under `face` in each `tools/surfer/presets/*.json`, and the built-in `game_engine` rig with its skin weights; then reshaped and trimmed by `tools/surfer/` | CC0 1.0: MakeHuman's base mesh, targets and exported characters are CC0 (https://static.makehumancommunity.org/about/license.html; closed-source use: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb/faq/use_in_closed_source.html) |
| Hair cards, eyes, boardies, wardrobe masks, Grommet's glasses and teeth, the pimple spots | Generated by `tools/surfer/hair.py`, `wardrobe.py`, `glasses.py`, `teeth.py` and `skin.py` (this project) | This project's own |
| Hair strands, skin (freckles, pimples, sunburn), lenses, teeth, neoprene and fabric shading | Procedural shaders in `src/surfer/surferShading.ts` (this project); no texture files | This project's own |
| Boards and swim fins | Generated by `src/board/` (this project) | This project's own |
