# Surfer assets: sources and licences

| Asset | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Body meshes and skin weights (`female.glb`, `male.glb`, `grommet.glb`) | Generated with MPFB 2.0.17 (MakeHuman) in Blender 5.2.2 LTS from MPFB's data: the base mesh, the macro targets (gender, age, muscle, weight, proportions, height), the face targets listed under `face` in each `tools/surfer/presets/*.json`, and the built-in `game_engine` rig with its skin weights; then reshaped and trimmed by `tools/surfer/`. The face's morph targets (blinks, jaw, smile, brows, squint, nostrils) are MPFB's expression units (`data/targets/expression/units/caucasian`), and the lashes are the base mesh's own eyelash helper strips, reshaped | CC0 1.0: MakeHuman's base mesh, targets and exported characters are CC0 (https://static.makehumancommunity.org/about/license.html; closed-source use: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb/faq/use_in_closed_source.html) |
| Hair cards (wet and dry styles), eyes, boardies, wardrobe masks, Grommet's glasses, everyone's teeth, the pimple spots, the breathing morph, the baked occlusion | Generated by `tools/surfer/hair.py`, `wardrobe.py`, `glasses.py`, `teeth.py`, `skin.py` and `expressions.py` (this project) | This project's own |
| Hair strands, skin (pores, blush, stubble, freckles, pimples, sunburn), eyes, lashes, lenses, teeth, neoprene and fabric shading | Procedural shaders in `src/surfer/surferShading.ts` (this project); no texture files | This project's own |
| Boards and swim fins | Generated by `src/board/` (this project) | This project's own |
| Walking clothes (tees, cutoffs, bikini straps, thongs, the cap and bucket hat, the hair under them), the packs, towel, wetsuit and clipped fins, and the beach pile (`beachPile.glb`) | Generated by `tools/surfer/clothes.py`, `packs.py`, `hair.py`, `geom.py` and `pile.py` from the bodies above (this project) | This project's own |
| Cloth shading (weave, creases, occlusion, worn and frayed denim, canvas, neoprene, rubber, towel stripes) | Procedural shader `clothMaterial` in `src/surfer/surferShading.ts` (this project); no texture files | This project's own |

The gang mockup's lettering (`src/dev/gangCard.ts`, a dev capture tool) uses Knewave (Tyler Finck) and Caveat Brush
(Impallari Type) from Google Fonts, both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. They are loaded from Google Fonts by the
dev tool and not bundled; if the select screen (step 4) adopts them, ship the font files with their OFL text.

MPFB's source code is GPL. It is a build tool only and is not shipped; the GPL does not reach the characters it exports.
