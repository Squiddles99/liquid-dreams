# Loading pictures: sea life of the Cape to Cape coast

For Andrew, 2026-10-08. Things that live in or around the water from Cape Naturaliste to Cape Leeuwin, for more loading
slides like the balga. I picked each one because it's real to this stretch of coast and because it has a story worth a
card. **I'll check every fact (and any Noongar name or use) against a source when its picture lands**, never from
memory, so anything below marked *(check)* may get dropped if it doesn't hold up.

Same prompt as the flora (prompt pack E), with the subject swapped and "underwater" where it fits:

> A [close-up nature / underwater] photograph of [SUBJECT] [in its setting], on the coast near Margaret River, Western
> Australia, soft natural light, shallow depth of field. The lower-left third of the frame is calm and uncluttered
> (soft background only). 16:9, as high a resolution as possible. No text, no logos, no watermark.
