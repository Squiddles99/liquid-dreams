## Ruling (step 2)
The only item at or over 20% of a sum is the field sample (21-25% of a sum, 24-25% of a waterAt). Its samples are all at
distinct points, so none can be dropped, but each sample locates its grid cell ten times (once per array): that repeat is
exact to remove (the same arithmetic, bit-identical), and measured at ~60% of a sample. Expected: ~14% of a waterAt, ~7% of
riding at 12 ft and ~5% at 6 ft. Nothing else reaches 20%: waveAtCrest's 19-20% is the shape arithmetic itself, the onset
record (14-15%) is read once per wave at distinct points.
