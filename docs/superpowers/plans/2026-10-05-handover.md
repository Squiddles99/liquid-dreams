## Why it keeps failing

1. **We can't see what he sees.** The cloud container has no working WebGPU capture (headless SwiftShader loses its
   device), and the CPU ray-marcher is crude and ignores shading. Checks were height maps and cross-sections, so shading
   seams pass unnoticed. Get a view of the GPU frame (or his moment links and screenshots) before claiming a fix.
2. **The wave is two surfaces stitched together.** The sheet is the analytic swell (SetWaves / setWaveModel: the 'lean'
   front and the bore). The ribbon is Andrew's keyframed 2D profile (wombProfile / wombSection), swept along crest
   stations and drawn by BreakingRibbon, blended into the sheet by ρ, interiorWeight, frontHeight and the seat. Every
   hand-over between them can show as a step, seam or wedge: across the wave, along the crest, and in shading. The
   current wedge is most likely the ribbon's footprint. Its mesh reaches 7 A in front of the crest. Past 4 A its heights
   are the sheet's, but it is shaded by its own rules (the chop added per vertex, its own normals), and it ends in a
   sharp edge.
3. **The approved drawing itself.** Its flats sat at still water, while the real water in front is the swell's trough;
   it is now seated on the trough (ac88408). Its lip also never touches the water: the tip stays 0.23–0.32 A above it.
   A proposed fix awaits Andrew's sign-off: in wombProfile.PROFILE_KEYS, the phase-1 hollow tip's y goes from −0.14 to
   −0.36. (Lowering the tip at phase 1.25 as well makes the curve cross itself at phase 1.4.) After sign-off, run
   `node tools/drawWombProfiles.ts --freeze`.
4. **The refraction field** (first-arrival eikonal, ray amplitude) makes caustic bands and kinks. A band of focused
   swell from the reef's corner runs through the take-off: the crest is 15% higher along it, with a ridge on the back.
5. **43 unit tests fail**, most pinned to the old reef, so the suite is not a reliable guard.
