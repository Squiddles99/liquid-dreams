The relaxation does what the spec said: 0.3 s after the catch she is at 0.85–0.95 c. Then gravity and the rail hold her

## Does the drop look like a drop?

In the bot's third-person frames, yes: `r15-8ft--3.0.png` has her crouched at the foot of a wall several times her
height, and `r15-6ft--3.0.png` stands her in the pocket under the curl. The live runs go almost straight into the tube
POV, so they show the barrel rather than the drop.

**In height, barely.** I have only `b.y` (board height against still water), not the trough, so this is a proxy: she
falls 0.15–0.5 m in the second after pop-up. The carry takes her *along* with the crest more than *down* the face. The
face rises around her rather than her falling down it. If Andrew says "it doesn't feel like a drop", this is why.

## What surprised me

1. **Beginner misses, intermediate and expert make it.** The softer catch (0.28 slope, assist 8) catches her 0.8 s before
   arrival, high on a still-gentle face. At ~0.9 c she is matched to the crest, rides its top (dy +0.6 m), and the lip
   passes under her. Higher forgiveness gave a worse result. The carry probably wants a position gate (she must be on the
   face, below the crest, before it takes her), not just slope ≥ catchSlope.
2. **The capture bot (pop-up on the catch frame) wipes out ~1 s after popping** at 6 and 8 ft. The keyed live run, popping
   0.2–0.3 s later, rides 12–15 s. So popping instantly is now *punished* (going by those two runs), which may be right
   for the game, but it's Review Focus 5's case and nobody has said that's intended. Cause not traced.
3. **The final review's one Important finding (not fixed, a design call for you):** the carry is two-way. It relaxes
   her *toward* 0.85 c, so it brakes as well as pushes. While carried, gravity on a 0.7 face only gets her ~0.7 m/s past
   0.85 c, and the along-the-line speed she sets during the pop-up decays ~93 % over 0.4 s (the relaxation pulls every
   component toward `dir × vc`). That fits the beginner miss: matched to the crest, she cannot outrun it down the face.
   The reviewer's fix: one-way along `dir` only, `dv = vc − v·dir; if (dv > 0) v += dir·dv·k`, drag target unchanged.
   The spec says "relaxes", so I kept it as written.
4. The full suite times out ~20 tests when run while the machine is loaded. They pass alone. That makes the baseline
   noisy: diff failing names on a quiet machine, file by file if needed.
