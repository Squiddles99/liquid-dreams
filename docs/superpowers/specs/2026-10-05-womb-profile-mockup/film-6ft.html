The left, slice by slice: 6 ft, mid tide

Each picture is the game's own slice of the wave at that place and moment (the station the game puts there, its shape from your approved drawings, set by the reef). Rows are seconds after the wave reaches the take-off; columns are places down the left. Viewpoint A: the beach is on the right. The small numbers: stage (0 swell, 0.5 pitching, 1 round barrel, 2 white water) and hollowness (1 = the Womb's barrel). Drawn 48 m across by 12.5 m tall, the same scale both ways.
