How should the Library lay out the 66 entries?

Same chart colours and fonts as the map. The leafy seadragon is the focused entry in each. A detail page (at a glance, the story, Noongar knowledge, did you know?) opens from any of them.

A · Field journal (my recommendation)

A naturalist’s notebook: each category is a coloured tab down the page edge; pictures are taped-in prints with handwritten names; the right page is the open entry. Warm and handmade, the same paper as the surf chart, and it reads like a book you want to flip through. Nine prints a page; a category of 15 is two pages.

C · Coast transect

The coast cut in a slice, forest to open ocean, every plant and animal placed where it lives. Filter chips pick a category; the open entry sits below. It teaches the place itself, not just a list, and it reads like the surf chart turned on its side. The catch: 66 medallions crowd the strip, so it would scroll sideways, and moving between round medallions with a controller is fiddly.
